About me

I work on physical AI and aerial robotics, training and deploying models that let robots perceive their surroundings, make decisions, and act in the physical world. My work spans reinforcement learning, vision-language navigation, and onboard learned perception.

I’m a Mechatronics Engineering student at EESC, University of São Paulo, and a researcher at the USP Center for Robotics (CROB). I build simulation environments, design rewards, and train and compare reinforcement-learning navigation policies. I also quantize and deploy vision networks, VLMs, and VLA baselines on embedded hardware, connecting learned components with state estimation, planning, and control.

My recent work includes VLN on the Fly, visual relocalization against 3D Gaussian-splatting reconstructions, and GPS-denied flight systems. Previously, I was a research intern at Texas A&M’s Advanced Vertical Flight Laboratory, an avionics intern at Embraer Defense, and director of aerial robotics at SEMEAR.

Research interests: physical AI; robot learning; reinforcement learning; vision-language-action models; aerial autonomy; onboard model deployment.

Selected projects

Onboard vision-language navigation

For VLN on the Fly, I deployed a quantized vision-language model onboard and integrated its image targets with depth to produce spatial navigation goals. I also deployed VLA baselines onboard and in simulation.

The stack combines learned grounding, EGO-Planner, and pretrained RAPTOR control. My RAPTOR work is deployment and integration, not policy training. The reported VLN flight experiments used motion-capture localization.

Reinforcement learning for confined-space flight

I built simulation environments, designed rewards, and trained and compared PPO and SAC policies for UAV navigation in procedurally generated industrial ducts. This work was evaluated in high-fidelity simulation and published at LARS 2025.

Visual relocalization with 3D Gaussian splatting

I developed visual relocalization by matching live-camera observations against a digital twin reconstructed from monocular video using 3D Gaussian splatting. The system provides odometry corrections and robot-position updates in ground control for the Petrobras / Embrapii project.

Warehouse coverage planning and inventory

For the Volkswagen project, I’m developing warehouse coverage planning using lidar-inertial odometry and map information. Previously, I worked on offboard vision-based box identification for UAV inventory.

GPS and vision-based flight transitions

At Texas A&M, I built an autonomous flight stack, implemented navigation nodes and visual-inertial odometry, and modified ArduPilot firmware to switch EKF estimation sources between flight modes. Field tests successfully demonstrated in-flight transitions between GPS and vision-based state estimation.

MIRA / IMAV 2026

Integrated pretrained RAPTOR control with lidar-inertial odometry, behavior-tree mission logic, and PX4 on the Mobile Robotics Group’s custom drone. The team placed fourth indoors and received the Most Promising Team award.
