Approach & experimental scope

A quantized VLM grounds an instruction into an image region; depth converts that grounding to a 3D goal. EGO-Planner generates a trajectory and the pretrained RAPTOR policy tracks it through PX4. A supervisor gates handoffs between stages. The reported flight experiments used motion-capture localization; onboard model execution does not imply onboard localization.

System & competition

The Mobile Robotics Group’s custom drone uses lidar-inertial odometry as its position source, behavior trees for mission logic, and a pretrained learned control policy inside the flight controller. The system was validated in simulation before flight. The team placed fourth indoors and received the Most Promising Team award at IMAV 2026.

03 Learned perception & estimation

Relocalization beyond odometry

Combining learned visual features with geometric estimation to correct accumulated drift against a prebuilt map.

My work Separating local VIO from global relocalization, then fusing map-based corrections in a GTSAM pose graph for navigation.

Learned featureshloc / COLMAPGTSAM
Approach & tools

Local estimation uses visual-inertial odometry. An offline mapping and relocalization pipeline explores SuperPoint, AnyLoc/DINOv2, and LightGlue with COLMAP. Global fixes are fused into the local estimate and passed to the flight controller. I also explored 3D Gaussian Splatting as an alternative map representation.

About me

I work on physical AI: training and deploying models that let robots perceive their surroundings, make decisions, and act in the physical world. My main focus is aerial robotics, with work spanning reinforcement learning, vision-language navigation, and onboard learned perception.

I connect model training and evaluation with deployment on embedded hardware, integrating learned components with state estimation, planning, and control. My projects include reinforcement-learning research in simulation and pretrained-model deployment on real drones.

I’m a Mechatronics Engineering student at EESC, University of São Paulo, and a researcher at the USP Center for Robotics, working on robot learning and autonomous systems.
